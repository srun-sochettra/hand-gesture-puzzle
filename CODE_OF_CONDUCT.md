# Code of conduct

Keep discussions respectful, constructive and focused on the work. Give specific feedback, accept corrections and make room for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, repeated unwanted contact and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not share footage of other people without permission.

For non-sensitive concerns, ask the maintainer for help in the relevant discussion. For private matters, request a private contact without publishing details. No dedicated reporting address or response deadline is established here.

Security concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behaviour and its impact. This document does not promise confidential reporting infrastructure or a fixed enforcement timetable.
